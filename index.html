homelab-assistant

A personal assistant for one person, running on their own home server.

homelab-assistant is a private, single-user project. It helps its owner keep up with day-to-day life by:

  • reading the owner's own Gmail accounts to flag messages that need attention,
  • writing draft replies that the owner reviews and sends themselves (it never sends email on its own), and
  • reading and adding events on the owner's Google Calendar for reminders and scheduling.

It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is used only by its owner.

See the Privacy Policy for exactly what data it accesses and how it is handled.
