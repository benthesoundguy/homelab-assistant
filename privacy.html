Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Who uses this app

homelab-assistant is a private, single-user application. Its only user is its owner, who connects their own Google accounts to it. It is not available to the public.

What Google data it accesses

  • Gmail: reads messages in the owner's accounts, and creates draft replies. It does not send email; sending is disabled in the software.
  • Google Calendar: reads events and creates or updates events on the owner's calendars.

How the data is used

Only to help the owner: to summarize and prioritize their messages, draft replies for the owner to review, and manage reminders and schedule. Data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone other than the processing described below.

Where the data goes

  • Access tokens and any stored data stay on the owner's private, self-hosted server, which is not reachable from the public internet.
  • To summarize messages and write drafts, message content may be processed by AI model providers that the owner selects, through the owner's own AI gateway. Providers are chosen with no-training or zero-retention terms where available.
  • No other person has access to the data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

homelab-assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect any account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops all access, and can delete the stored data from their server.
